What CityFee does

City dropdownThe free-text city field becomes a list of the cities you deliver to, per country, for billing and shipping addresses.
Delivery fee per cityThe "Delivery by city" shipping method charges the fee of the chosen city, with a default fee and "not delivered" cities.
Classic and block checkoutWorks in the classic WooCommerce checkout and the Checkout block. The fee updates when the customer changes the city.
Arabic and French readyTranslation-ready and right-to-left ready. French and Arabic translations have been prepared.

How it works

  1. Go to WooCommerce > CityFee, choose a country and add or paste your cities with their fees.
  2. In WooCommerce > Settings > Shipping, add "Delivery by city" to the zone that covers the country and set the default fee.
  3. Customers choose their city from the list and see its delivery fee at checkout.
