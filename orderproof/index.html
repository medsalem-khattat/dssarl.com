How it works

  1. A customer places a Cash on Delivery order.
  2. The order waits in "Awaiting WhatsApp verification". It holds no stock and no "New order" email is sent yet.
  3. The customer gets a 6-digit code on WhatsApp and types it on the verification page.
  4. The order becomes a real order: stock is reserved and your usual emails go out.
  5. Orders that are not verified are cancelled automatically after the number of hours you choose (2 by default).

To send real codes you need your own WhatsApp Business number on Meta's WhatsApp Cloud API and an approved message template of the Authentication category. The settings page guides you step by step, and "Send test code" checks the setup.
